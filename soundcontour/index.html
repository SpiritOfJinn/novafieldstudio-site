Help & FAQ

How much does it cost?

Sound Contour is free to download with a 7-day free trial. After that, it's a one-time $9.99 purchase. There's no subscription.

I already bought it. How do I unlock it on another Mac?

Open Sound Contour, go to Settings, and click Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple Account you bought it with.

The equalizer isn't changing my audio.

macOS needs to give Sound Contour permission to process system audio. Open Sound Contour's Settings and follow the permission prompt, or check System Settings > Privacy & Security.

Does the iPhone or iPad app work on its own?

No. It's a remote control for Sound Contour on your Mac. It doesn't change audio playing on your iPhone or iPad. Your Mac must be running Sound Contour on the same Wi-Fi network.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com.
